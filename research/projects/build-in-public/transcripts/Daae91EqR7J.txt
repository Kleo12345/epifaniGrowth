The fastest way to change your life is to rip yourself out of your physical and digital environment. You just change everything overnight. You stop living the same day over and over on repeat. You change the places you go, the accounts you follow, the information that you consume, the books that you read, and if you don't read books, you start because you naturally grow to fit the environment that you throw yourself into. It's going to be difficult and extremely uncomfortable at first to be around new people, especially if the people are much further ahead than you. But if you're able to stick with that vulnerability as you grow into that new shell, then that is exactly how you change your life extremely fast.
