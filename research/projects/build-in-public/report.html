6 of 71 posts use a "comment X and I'll send it" style CTA. Not dominant here, but the posts that use it tend to over-perform.

Hook analysis

Of the top 6: 2 open with a hook under 60 characters, 3 put a specific number in the first line. The winning openers state a concrete outcome, problem, or contrarian claim — none open with a greeting or context-setting.
