Today I officially launched my app SuperDefit, which is a very simple app. Most fitness apps seem designed for your grandma or doing the rock Johnson, with literally no in between. Then it was only ever manually enter all your meals or take a photo that was never accurate. SuperDefit, let's just say whatever you ate or without that day to remove all the friction on staying consistent and actually getting lean. But at the end of every week you had a fitness wrap, showing you your fun stats and slight tweaks you need to make to stay on track. It's still in the early stages and there's many more features to come, but if you simply want to get lean and build more muscle disorder without stressing over every calorie, then check it out on the app store.
